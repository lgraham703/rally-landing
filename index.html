How It Works
Simple by design.
1

Create an Event

Name your moment — a hospital stay, a wedding weekend, a family emergency. Set it up in seconds.

2

Build Your Groups

Add contacts and organize them into groups. Close family, extended network, coworkers — whoever needs to know.

3

Send Updates

Our Story
Built from a real moment of need.

I had packed my bags for a ski weekend and planned an early morning workout with a friend before hitting the road. By that afternoon, I was in the ER.

What started as a stomachache turned into a battery of tests, emergency surgery, and a 10-day hospital stay. With everything happening so fast, I had no good way to keep the people who mattered most in the loop. Phone calls were exhausting. Group texts fell apart. I needed something that didn't exist yet.

"My medical team was exceptional, but they couldn't be everything. The support of friends and family outside those hospital walls was crucial to my recovery."

We live far from each other, and in moments that move fast, we need technology that keeps up. Rally is that tool — whether you're navigating a health crisis, coordinating a wedding weekend, or managing any life moment that involves more people than a text thread can handle.

— Lexi, Founder
